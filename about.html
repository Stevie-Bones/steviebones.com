About Stevie Bones

Barett and Cassandra Bjorson bring a balanced blend of precision and creativity to both new construction and renovation projects. Barett's engineering background supports a detail-oriented and efficient build process, while Cassandra leads design with a thoughtful, creative approach shaped by her life and industry experience.

Stevie Bones is rooted in collected simplicity and guided by a refined sense of restraint, honoring craftsmanship, embracing character, and bringing quiet luxury into everyday living.
