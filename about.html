About Stevie Bones

Barett and Cassandra Bjorson bring a balanced blend of precision and creativity to both new construction and renovation projects. Barett's engineering background supports a detail-oriented and efficient building process, while Cassandra leads design with a thoughtful, creative approach shaped by her life and industry experience.

Together, they offer a full-service approach—guiding each project from initial concept through construction and into the final interior details—to create cohesive, well-crafted homes.
